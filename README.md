# mylittlegarden
A fun little project that I made where everyone can show their hidden artist without revealing themselves.

Later merged into a single file so that its easy to maintain later.

🌐 Visit: https://suryanshkapoor.qd.je

Why I made this
Most places on the internet ask us to post something, get attention, and see how people react. I wanted to make a small place that works a little differently.

Here, you draw a tree. It doesn't have to be good, clever, or even look much like a tree. You plant it, and it becomes part of a garden made by people you may never meet. There are no likes, rankings, profiles, or follower counts. Just a collection of little drawings left by people who happened to stop by.

It's a simple project, but I like the thought of all those small, different contributions sitting together in one place.
What you can do
- Draw on a blank canvas using the pen, eraser, and fill tool.
- Pick from 12 colours.
- Undo a stroke or clear the canvas and start again.
- Plant your drawing in the shared garden.
- Browse trees left by other visitors.
The garden loads drawings in batches and checks for new contributions every few seconds while it is open.
A note about the drawings
The drawings are made by visitors, not generated by AI. You don't need an account to plant a tree. The project doesn't ask for your name or show dates beside your drawing. Once planted, a drawing becomes part of the public garden and can be seen by other visitors.
How it works
The front end is a small HTML, CSS, and JavaScript page. The drawing itself is made on an HTML canvas and exported as a PNG.

When someone plants a tree, the page sends that PNG to a PHP endpoint. The image is saved in the uploads/ folder, and its file path is stored in a MySQL table. When someone visits the garden, another PHP endpoint reads the paths from the database and returns the drawings to the page.

The images live on the server, not in an individual visitor's browser. That is what makes it one shared garden.
